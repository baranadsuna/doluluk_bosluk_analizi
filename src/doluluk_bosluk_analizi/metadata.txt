about=Figure-Ground & Building Coverage Analyzer makes it simple to analyze urban built density and open space ratios. Whether you are an urban planner, architect, GIS analyst, or researcher, this tool helps you evaluate spatial enclosure, building coverage ratios (BCR / TAKS), and open space permeability.
 Features:
 - Universal Calculation: Works in both Spatial Overlay mode (aggregating building polygons into zones with boundary-containment) and Direct Attribute mode (single-layer calculation).
 - Adaptive Nolli and Heatmap Symbology: Automatically styles output maps with balanced 5-class graduated colors (Urban Warm Heatmap, Nolli Grayscale, or Eco-Traffic).
 - Instant KPI Dashboard: Displays overall coverage percentage, void ratio, building count, and density metrics.
 - Export Ready: One-click export to GeoPackage, shapefile, CSV, and clipboard.
 - 4 Languages with Live Switching: English, Turkish, Chinese, and Indonesian.
 - High Performance: Fast spatial indexing powered by QgsSpatialIndex.
 - QGIS 3 and QGIS 4 / Qt5 and Qt6 Ready.
